## Voiceover (record clean, slow, one take per line)

1. In 2020 I wrote down eight things I had started.

4. Cooking: only when I was in the mood.

7. Then I wrote this: "Why can't I complete even one of the things I had started?"
8. Six years later I still don't have the answer.
9. But I stopped needing a mood.
10. One reel. Every day. For 100 days.
11. Day 1. Hold me to it.
